About me

I'm a PhD Student at the Harvard Quantum Initiative, advised by Jordan Cotler. I work on theoretical physics, quantum computing, and machine learning. Most recently, I've been developing applications of quantum processors to experimental science and future technology.

Prior to Harvard I studied physics and computer science at Caltech, where I worked in John Preskill's group under Hsin-Yuan Huang and Leo Zhou.
